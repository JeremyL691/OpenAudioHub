OpenAudioHub is a self-hosted web app for Plaud recordings. It syncs your recordings from your Plaud account, keeps the audio on storage you control, and produces transcripts and summaries with the AI providers you choose. When you decide to leave, you can export everything.

Plaud Note, Note Pro, and NotePin are supported today.

## Using OpenAudioHub

These guides cover the day-to-day features.

- **[Connect your Plaud account](/docs/guides/connect-plaud-account)**: sign in with an email code, or use the browser connector for Google and Apple accounts.
- **[AI providers](/docs/guides/ai-providers)**: configure OpenAI, Groq, Google Gemini, ElevenLabs, local models, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
- **[Notifications](/docs/guides/notifications)**: Bark, browser notifications, and email.
- **[Automation API and webhooks](/docs/guides/automation-and-webhooks)**: personal API keys and signed webhook deliveries.
- **[Backup and restore](/docs/guides/backup-and-restore)**: export every recording, transcript, and summary so you can leave.

## How it ships

OpenAudioHub is AGPL-3.0 software, and it is self-hosted only. The source and releases are in the [repository](https://github.com/JeremyL691/OpenAudioHub). Existing installations can follow the [migration guide](https://github.com/JeremyL691/OpenAudioHub/blob/main/docs/MIGRATION_FROM_RIFFADO.md).
