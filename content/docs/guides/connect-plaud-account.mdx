---
title: Connect your Plaud account
description: Sign in to Plaud from OpenAudioHub, including the Google and Apple workaround.
---

OpenAudioHub signs into Plaud directly, the same way the official Plaud app does. It does not scrape pages or use a headless browser. The flow you use depends on how you created your Plaud account.

## Email and code (default)

If you signed up to Plaud with an email address and password:

1. Enter the email address you use on [plaud.ai](https://plaud.ai).
2. Plaud emails you a six-digit code.
3. Enter the code in OpenAudioHub.

The code goes to Plaud's servers and is not stored. The access token that comes back is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is saved. See [Encryption at rest](/docs/reference/encryption-at-rest).

OpenAudioHub detects your Plaud region automatically. If Plaud reports that your account lives on another regional server, it follows that redirect and stores the region with the token.

## Signed up with Google or Apple

If you signed up to Plaud with **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**, the email-and-code flow can appear to succeed and then sync zero recordings. Plaud treats the Google or Apple identity as a separate account, even when the email address matches.

Plaud's Google sign-in cannot be used directly from OpenAudioHub, so there are two workarounds.

### Browser connector (recommended)

Install the browser connector extension, then choose **Sign in with Plaud** on the connect screen. Sign in to web.plaud.ai the way you normally do. The extension sends the resulting token to your OpenAudioHub instance, so you do not copy anything. The connector is a separate AGPL-3.0 project. Its source and release links are in the [NOTICE](https://github.com/JeremyL691/OpenAudioHub/blob/main/NOTICE) file.

### Paste a token

If you cannot install the extension:

1. Open [web.plaud.ai](https://web.plaud.ai) and sign in.
2. Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Console, and run `localStorage.getItem('pld_tokenstr')`.
3. Copy the long `eyJ...` value it prints.
4. In OpenAudioHub, open the **Paste token** tab, choose the region your account is on, and paste the value.

Do not copy a token from a network request header. Those are short-lived workspace tokens. They expire within about a day, and OpenAudioHub rejects them during connection.

The pasted token is encrypted at rest in the same way as a token from the email flow.

## Refresh tokens

Plaud does not issue refresh tokens. The token lasts roughly 300 days. When it expires, the recordings library shows a reconnect banner, and you reconnect with the same method you used before. Reconnecting keeps your existing recordings.

## Switching accounts

Disconnect or switch the Plaud account under **Settings → Plaud Account**. The stored token is deleted. Recordings you already synced stay in your storage.
